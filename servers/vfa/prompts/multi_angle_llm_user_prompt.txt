### Classification Logic:
- **Logical Operators**: Uppercase "AND"/"OR" are used consistently throughout
  - "AND": ALL connected elements must be satisfied simultaneously
  - "OR": At least ONE of the connected elements must be satisfied
- **Spatial Evidence**: Contact-based actions require specific visual proof of touch

## 3. CLASSIFICATION WORKFLOW

1. **Follow Decision Process**: Apply the step-by-step rule-based if-else chain above
2. **Stop at First Match**: Use the first step in the decision process that is satisfied
3. **Verify Contact Evidence** (when required):
   - Fingertips/palm visibly touching surfaces
   - Hand occluding/overlapping target object
   - Object deformation (bent wires, moved papers)
   - Clear grasping posture with engaged fingers
4. **Multi-angle Analysis**: Consider information from ALL camera perspectives
5. **Evidence Priority**: Direct observations over inferences when possible
