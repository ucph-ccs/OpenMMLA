### 3. Classification Approach:
- Focus primarily on two key elements for classification:
  1. Gaze Focus - what the person is looking at (target of gaze line)
  2. Hands Status - what the hands are doing/touching (or inferred to be doing if partially obscured)
- Consider information from ALL available camera perspectives to make the most accurate assessment
- When one perspective provides clear information that another doesn't, prioritize the clearer view
- Classify each person based on the ACTION DEFINITIONS provided above
- Consider both direct observations and clearly marked inferences in the observations
- The combination of where someone is looking and what their hands are doing should determine classification
- Provide detailed justification for each classification, explicitly referencing the observed gaze and hand status from specific camera views
